ARTWORK 1: Sunset Painting

The sunset painting captures the beauty of nature as the sun slowly sets beyond the horizon. Warm shades of orange, red, and yellow blend with soft purples and blues, creating a calm and peaceful mood. This artwork reflects the harmony between light and shadow, reminding us of the beauty in everyday moments


ARTWORK 2: Abstract Design

Mandala art is a spiritual and ritual symbol that represents the universe. The word Mandala comes from the ancient Indian language Sanskrit, meaning “circle.” Mandalas are created using intricate patterns, shapes, and symbols arranged in a circular form. They often begin with a central point and expand outward in layers, symbolizing unity, harmony, and balance


ARTWORK 3: Nature Painting

The nature painting shows the calm and refreshing beauty of the outdoors. Green trees, tall mountains, and flowing rivers come together to create a peaceful scene. It reflects the purity of nature and gives a sense of relaxation and harmony.
